## Engineering Manager
### Role Summary
Oversees engineering team capacity, development practices, and delivery capability.

### Responsibilities
- Manage engineering capacity, staffing, and technical delivery risks.
- Support developers' growth and remove organizational blockers.
- Coordinate engineering dependencies with Project Managers.

### Goals
- Maintain sustainable delivery and engineering quality.
- Balance delivery commitments with technical health.

### Typical Communication
- Engineering planning and delivery reviews.
- Regular coordination with Project Managers and Product Managers.

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Developers: provides coaching and resolves resource constraints.
- Project Managers: aligns staffing, timelines, and delivery risks.
- Product Managers: discusses roadmap feasibility and trade-offs.
- Stakeholders: escalates significant engineering constraints.

## Design / UX Lead
### Role Summary
Owns user experience consistency, research, and design direction.

### Responsibilities
- Translate user needs into flows, prototypes, and interface specifications.
- Coordinate usability testing and accessibility reviews.
- Maintain design standards and support design handoffs.

### Goals
- Deliver accessible, usable, and consistent experiences.
- Reduce usability-related rework.

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews, usability findings, and developer handoffs.
- Regular product discovery and backlog refinement discussions.

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Product Managers: validates user needs and feature priorities.
- Developers: clarifies designs and implementation details.
- Project Managers: communicates design dependencies and timelines.
- Stakeholders: presents user research and design decisions.

## QA / Test Lead
### Role Summary
Coordinates testing strategy, release validation, and quality risks.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans and coverage across unit, integration, and end-to-end testing.
- Coordinate defect triage, regression testing, and acceptance verification.
- Report quality risks and release readiness.

### Goals
- Reduce escaped defects and improve confidence in releases.
- Ensure acceptance criteria are verifiably met.

### Typical Communication
- Test status reports, defect triage, and release readiness reviews.
- Feedback through pull requests and issue trackers.

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Developers: coordinates defect resolution and test automation.
- Product Managers: clarifies acceptance criteria.
- Project Managers: communicates quality risks and release blockers.
- Stakeholders: provides relevant acceptance and readiness evidence.

## Security / Compliance Partner
### Role Summary
Advises the team on security controls, privacy, and compliance requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Review security and compliance requirements during planning.
- Coordinate threat assessments, vulnerability remediation, and control validation.
- Document security risks and required approvals.

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance exposure.
- Identify risks early without creating unnecessary delivery delays.

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews, risk assessments, and compliance checkpoints.
- Escalations for critical vulnerabilities or control gaps.

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Developers: provides secure implementation guidance.
- Product Managers: clarifies security-related product requirements.
- Project Managers: tracks security dependencies and risks.
- Stakeholders: communicates compliance readiness and residual risk.

## Technical Lead / Architect
### Role Summary
Guides technical architecture and cross-component engineering decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define architecture principles and technical design direction.
- Review designs, interfaces, dependencies, and technical risks.
- Support engineers in resolving complex implementation challenges.

### Goals
- Maintain scalable, secure, and maintainable architecture.
- Reduce technical risk and integration problems.

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews, technical decision records, and design discussions.
- Cross-team technical planning and code review feedback.

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Developers: guides implementation and technical decisions.
- Product Managers: explains technical trade-offs and feasibility.
- Project Managers: identifies architectural dependencies and delivery risks.
- Stakeholders: communicates important architectural implications.
